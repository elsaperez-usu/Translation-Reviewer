Translation Review Instrument

For healthcare professionals reviewing translated patient forms. Log each issue with its page and segment, rate how serious it is, and suggest a better wording.

How to complete this review

  1. Fill in your professional background and the form you are reviewing.
  2. Read the translated form page by page. For each problem, copy the segment exactly as it appears, add the page number, and choose a category and severity.
  3. Write your suggested edit and, if helpful, a short reason. One issue per entry.
  4. Give the form an overall rating, then submit. Your work saves as a draft while you go.

Severity levels

CriticalCould affect patient safety, consent, or a care decision. Must be fixed before use.
MajorCould confuse patients or lead to an incomplete or wrong answer on the form.
MinorMeaning is clear, but wording, style, or spelling could be improved.
